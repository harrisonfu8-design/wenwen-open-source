# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable, technical feedback about code and behavior, not about people.
- Respect privacy: do not post another person’s files, invoice data, credentials,
  or private paths in issues or discussions.
- Accept correction and keep discussion focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, fake engagement, or manipulating stars, forks, reviews, or rankings.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies in repository issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, and
other project spaces, and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainer through the contact method
listed on the maintainer’s GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly.
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or temporarily or
permanently ban participants for behavior that violates this code.
